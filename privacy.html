Privacy Policy

VITAP Hostel Mess

Last updated: October 19, 2026

VITAP Hostel Mess is an independent student project and is not officially affiliated with or endorsed by VIT-AP University.

1. No account or sign-in

The app does not require you to create an account or sign in. There is no username, password, or personal profile associated with using the app.

2. Menu data

The app displays hostel mess menu information (hostel, menu type, meal times, and dishes). This data is:

  • Fetched over a secure (HTTPS) connection from a public data source maintained by the developer
  • Cached on your device so menus remain viewable without an internet connection
  • Not linked to your identity in any way — menu requests do not include any personal information

3. On-device settings

The app stores a small set of preferences locally on your device (your selected hostel, menu type, theme, and reminder settings). This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to the developer or any third party.

4. Notifications

If you enable meal reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device using Android's notification system. No data about these reminders is sent off your device.

5. Advertising

The app displays ads served through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use your device's advertising ID. This is used only for displaying and measuring ads, not for any other purpose by this app.

You can control ad personalization and reset your advertising ID at any time in your device's Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization" or "Reset advertising ID" — wording differs by Android version).

Google's own privacy practices for AdMob are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Data sharing

Other than the advertising ID described above, the app does not share, sell, or transmit any data to third parties. The app does not collect sensitive data such as your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or financial information.

7. Children

This app is intended for VIT-AP University hostel residents, who are predominantly aged 16 and above. The app does not knowingly direct advertising at or collect data from young children, and does not contain content designed for children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised. Material changes will be reflected here before taking effect.
